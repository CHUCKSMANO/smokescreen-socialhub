One hub for your apps

Pinned services from the SocialHub catalog. Shortcuts match the native app: ⌘1–⌘5 on macOS, Ctrl+1–Ctrl+5 elsewhere.

Signal deliberately has no web client in this hub, matching the upstream project's security position: the desktop app is the supported route.
